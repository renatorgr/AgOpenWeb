# GPS and NTRIP scenarios: the careful and the careless operator

AgOpenWeb's whole backend runs in-process with its real UDP service on port 9999 and its real NTRIP client. A fake caster (NTRIP 2.0 chunked and 1.0, 401 for a wrong login, the source table for an unknown mount point, a VRS mount point that waits for a GGA) sends an RTCM 3 epoch every second (MSM7 for four systems, base position every 10 s) through a fake internet that can drop silently, refuse, flap or switch networks. An emulated receiver takes what AgOpenWeb forwards to UDP 2233 (whole messages with a good checksum; datagrams over 512 bytes or closer than 10 ms count as lost on an AiO v26), keeps RTK fixed while its corrections are at most 10 s old, float up to 30 s, then single, and sends $PAOGI or $PANDA at 10 Hz. A steer module stub sends PGN 253. The tractor drives north at 8 km/h on an AB line with sections on Auto and AutoSteer on. Everything runs in real time; the screen is sampled every 100 ms.

## Findings (each kind once, most serious first)

1. **Improvement** `CORRECTIONS-UNUSED-SILENT` ×1: for 45 s the NTRIP status said "Connected to 127.0.0.1:43927/VRS3" while the receiver reported no correction age and stayed single: the corrections were not reaching it. AgOpenWeb knows both facts (it forwards 1.4 KB/s and the receiver's GGA has an empty age) but nothing says "the receiver is not using the corrections"; the Network IO line shows "correction age —" without the red mark it gets past 5 s. AgIO does not warn either
   Seen in: K7
2. **Improvement** `JUMP-HEADING-SPIKE` ×1: a 1 m sideways jump turned AgOpenWeb's heading by up to 57° (the tractor drove straight); steer angle up to 35.0°
   Seen in: C11
3. **Improvement** `MOUNT-CASE-HINT` ×1: the caster has "VRS3" and the operator typed "vrs3"; mount points are case-sensitive, and the message did not point at the near miss: "Mount point not found (the caster sent its source table)"
   Seen in: K2
